# TradeReach approved fact bank

Every claim in a post must come from this list. Source: the public site tradereach.net (read 10 Nov 2026).
Edit this file to change what posts may say. Anything not here must not be stated as fact.

## What it is
- Missed-call text-back for Australian trades (plumbers, electricians and other tradies).
- When a call isn't answered, the caller gets a text back within moments. The first text says who it's from and that it's an automated reply.
- It asks what they need. Customers reply in plain words; it picks up name, suburb, problem and a good time to call, and only asks for what's missing. Typos and slang ("dunny", "arvo") are fine.
- The tradie gets one clear job summary on their phone, plus an alert straight away if it's an emergency. Every job and conversation is kept in a dashboard.
- Dashboard lead statuses: New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost. Call back or reply by text from the dashboard.

## Your number
- Keep your number. Switch on call forwarding for calls you don't answer, where your carrier supports it. Calls you answer work as they do now.
- Every plan includes its own TradeReach phone number. (Do not mention prices.)

## Emergencies
- When a caller describes an emergency, the tradie gets an urgent alert before the details are finished.
- The caller gets fixed safety wording, approved by a licensed plumber before any real customer sees it. It is never improvised.
- Emergency types: burst pipes and flooding, gas smells, sewage overflows, sparking/smoke/exposed wires, storm and roof leaks.

## Per-trade questions
- Plumbing: problem, urgency, after-hours and callback time.
- Electrical: whole or part of the property without power; email for installs.
- Roofing: single or double storey; email for restorations; photos taken from the ground.
- Air conditioning: split, ducted or other; brand if mentioned.
- Patios and pergolas: rough size, attached or freestanding, email for the quote, photos.
- Carpentry: deck size, email, photos.
- Landscaping: rough area, email, photos.
- Painters, cleaners, handymen and others: general profile, matched to the services they list.

## Rules it follows (you stay in charge)
- It collects details. The tradie quotes, books and calls back.
- Never books a job or says one is booked.
- Never quotes a price.
- Never promises a callback time.
- Always says it's automated in the first text.
- STOP means stop. Opted-out numbers get nothing more.
- Every outgoing text is checked against fixed rules before it's sent; anything that breaks them is blocked.

## Demo and calculator (calls to action)
- Live demo at tradereach.net: no signup, runs in the browser, no real calls or texts, nothing typed is saved. Fictional business "Smith Plumbing".
- Missed-call calculator: missed calls per week × 52 ÷ 12 × average job value × conversion rate. Results are hypothetical, not a prediction or guarantee. The site's starting values are placeholders, not industry averages: never quote them as typical.

## Never say
- Customer numbers, testimonials, reviews, results, revenue recovered, percentages or industry statistics.
- Prices, discounts, free trials, contract terms.
- "AI" capability claims (the product is rule-based).
- That it books jobs, quotes, answers calls by voice, or guarantees anything.
- Anything about other businesses or people by name.
